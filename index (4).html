FAQ

Common questions

How much does a Treatment cost? expand_more
Our curations start at $2,500. The final cost depends on the complexity of the event, venue rentals, required vendors, and location. We provide a full transparent breakdown before any commitment.
How much notice do you need? expand_more
Ideally, we need 4-6 weeks to plan and execute a seamless experience. For more complex requests involving international travel or rare bookings, 2-3 months is preferred.
Do you handle photography? expand_more
Yes. Professional, discreet documentation (photo and/or video) is included in almost all of our packages, as capturing the memory is a core part of the Treatment.
What if I just want a simple dinner? expand_more
We specialize in curated, multi-layered surprise experiences. If you are just looking for a restaurant reservation or a standard party planner, we might not be the best fit.
